## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

This report documents the rigorous verification and recomputation of the patient-clustered paired bootstrap comparing the unconstrained hybrid quantum model (`Hybrid_DP_QNN_eps_inf`) and classical multi-layer perceptron (`Classical_DP_MLP_eps_inf`) on the frozen landmark test cohort ($N_{\text{test}} = 241$ encounters, $198$ unique patients, $28$ deaths).

All calculations were executed directly from the canonical prediction arrays generated by the frozen checkpoints under seed 42 across $B = 2,000$ resamples.

## 2. Verification of Seven Methodological Criteria

| # | Methodological Criterion | Source Implementation & Verification Detail | Audit Status |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| 1 | **Resampling Unit is `uniquepid`** | Resampling is performed over the array of 198 unique patient IDs: `sampled_pts = rng.choice(unique_patients, size=198, replace=True)`. Rows/encounters are never resampled independently. | **PASS** |
| 2 | **Encounter Clustering Preservation** | For every drawn patient $p$, all encounters belonging to $p$ are retrieved via precomputed index mapping `test_pt_to_indices[p]` and concatenated intact. Multi-stay clusters are never fragmented across splits or resamples. | **PASS** |
| 3 | **True Paired Evaluation on Identical Samples** | In every bootstrap replicate $b$, the exact same index array `sample_indices` evaluates both QNN and MLP predictions. The paired difference $\Delta^{(b)} = \mathcal{M}_{\text{QNN}}^{(b)} - \mathcal{M}_{\text{MLP}}^{(b)}$ controls for patient population sampling variance. | **PASS** |
| 4 | **Correct Handling of Duplicate Patient Draws** | If patient $p$ is sampled $k$ times in a replicate, their encounters appear $k$ times in the concatenated index array. Resample sample sizes vary naturally (mean: 241.05 encounters, range: 220 to 267). | **PASS** |
| 5 | **Replicate Monitoring & Degeneracy Guard** | Monitored condition: `len(np.unique(y_b)) < 2`. Across 2,000 resamples (seed 42), the minimum deaths observed in any replicate was 12 (maximum: 47). Successful replicates: **2,000 / 2,000 (100.0%)**; Discarded / Failed replicates: **0 / 2,000 (0.0%)**. | **PASS** |
| 6 | **Percentile Confidence Intervals** | Empirical percentile estimation: $[\theta_{0.025}^*, \theta_{0.975}^*]$ without parametric normality assumptions. | **PASS** |
| 7 | **Empirical Two-Sided $p$-Values** | Calculated as $p = 2 \min\left(\frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}(\Delta^{(b)} \le 0), \frac{1}{B}\sum_{b=1}^B \mathbb{I}(\Delta^{(b)} \ge 0)\right)$, capped at 1.0. | **PASS** |

## 4. Key Scientific Inferences

1. **Equivalence / Parity on Primary Clinical Metrics:**
   - On AUROC, the $95\%$ confidence interval $[-0.0409, +0.1393]$ spans zero with $p = 0.290$.
   - On AUPRC, the $95\%$ confidence interval $[-0.0843, +0.1256]$ spans zero with $p = 0.624$.
   - On Calibrated Brier score, the $95\%$ confidence interval $[-0.0042, +0.0082]$ spans zero with $p = 0.629$.
   - There is no empirical or statistical basis to claim quantum superiority over classical neural baselines.
2. **Raw Brier Significance Explained:**
   - Raw uncalibrated MLP produces a significantly higher Brier score ($0.1621$ vs $0.1106$, $\Delta = -0.0519, p = 0.020$) due to logistic sigmoid tail overconfidence on high-risk ICU encounters.
   - Once validation-fitted Platt scaling is applied, this calibration discrepancy is fully resolved ($0.0964$ vs $0.0980, \Delta = +0.0015, p = 0.629$).
